Town Overview

Life in Puerto Vallarta

The mountains meet the bay around a working Pacific city. Puerto Vallarta offers beaches, neighborhood life and extensive visitor services, with plenty of settings beyond the resort strip.

The Appeal

Puerto Vallarta is a working Pacific city with a large visitor economy, municipal services and a mountains-to-bay setting.

Why It Made the Shortlist

Puerto Vallarta is a real working city, not only a resort: a palm-lined malecón with bronze sculptures, the Zona Romántica beach at Los Muertos, coves such as Mismaloya and mountains rising behind the bay.

Worth Knowing

Before you buy, check:

  • Security: the current Jalisco advisory and how neighborhoods vary
  • Climate and ground: cyclone and rainfall exposure, steep roads, and landslide and flood screening
  • Practicalities: water, insurance, permitted use and visitor economics
At a Glance

Before You Decide

  • Check the Jalisco security advisory, cyclones, flooding, landslides, water and insurance. Permitted property use and rental costs need current evidence.
