St Andrews

The Cathedral Town on the North Sea, Home of Golf and Scotland's Oldest University

Fife, Scotland · Coastal Cathedral Town and Home of Golf
The Pitch

A small medieval town on a headland over two sandy bays, with a ruined cathedral, a clifftop castle, Scotland's oldest university and the Old Course, about 1 h 10 from Edinburgh airport and its US flights

Why It's No. 6

The Scottish entry and the best US air link on the list after Winchester, in one of the drier, sunnier corners of Scotland, with an international name that sells itself

The Honest Cost

Some of the highest prices in Scotland, an 8 percent Additional Dwelling Supplement for a buyer who keeps a US home, a town shaped by students and golf, and coastal erosion on the sandy frontages

Getting There

Edinburgh airport about 1 h 10 by road, with year-round nonstops to Newark, New York and Washington; Leuchars station nearby, Edinburgh from 55 min

THE SCORECARD

Why St Andrews Ranks Sixth

St Andrews is the Scottish choice, and it brings things no English town on this list can. It is a small, intensely historic town of about 16,500 people on a headland above the North Sea, with the ruins of what was the largest cathedral in medieval Scotland, a castle on the cliff, a university founded in 1413, two beaches and the most famous golf course in the world. It sits in one of the drier and sunnier parts of Scotland, and it is about 1 h 10 by road from Edinburgh airport, which has a genuine choice of nonstop flights to the US. The terms are money and the sea. St Andrews is among the most expensive towns in Scotland; a US buyer who keeps a home in America pays Scotland's 8 percent Additional Dwelling Supplement on the whole price, which at typical prices makes buying here dearer than in England despite Scotland having no non-resident surcharge; the town's rhythm is set by students and golfers; and the dunes and sandy frontages face erosion that must be checked. For a buyer who wants the coast, the history and an easy flight home, it is a singular place.

A detail worth knowing

The opening scene of the film Chariots of Fire, the line of young athletes running barefoot through the surf to Vangelis's music, was filmed on the West Sands at St Andrews. The beach, about two miles of wide, flat sand running north from the town beside the golf links, is still where the town goes to walk, run and fly kites, and the view back along it to the spires and towers of the old town is one of the great seaside views in Britain.

The Five Filters

St Andrews, Read Five Ways

Natural Hazard

MEDIUM. Low seismic hazard and a relatively dry climate. The live questions are the sea: the dunes that protect the golf links are eroding, and in 2025 St Andrews Links Trust built a new 200-metre dune with 28,500 tonnes of sand on the estuary side; the Scottish Environment Protection Agency maps the East Sands and the Bruce Embankment in its 1-in-200-year coastal flood zone, and the Kinness Burn in its river flood zone. The old town on its headland is a different proposition from the low ground by the sands.

Access

HIGH for the US, MEDIUM otherwise. Edinburgh airport, about 1 h 10 by road, has year-round nonstops to Newark, New York JFK and Washington Dulles and seasonal ones to Boston, Atlanta, Chicago, Philadelphia and Orlando. There is no station in town; Leuchars, a few miles away, has frequent trains to Edinburgh and direct services to London, a long ride of about five and a half hours.

Scenery and Culture

HIGH. Cathedral ruins, castle, university quadrangles, the Old Course, two beaches and the Fife Coastal Path, with the fishing villages of the East Neuk a short drive south. A setting nothing else on the list matches.

Rental Viability

HIGH. Golf visitors from around the world, university families and graduation weeks, summer holidaymakers, and a strong student and academic long-let market. Short-term letting in Scotland requires a licence.

Practical Living

MEDIUM. Good shops and services for a small town, but the nearest A&E is in Dundee, about 30 minutes, and bigger shopping means Dundee or Edinburgh.
