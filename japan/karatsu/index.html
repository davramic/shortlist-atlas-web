Karatsu

A Castle on the Bay and a Living Pottery Tradition

Image & Source Notes ↗
Town Overview

Life in Karatsu

Karatsu has a castle above the bay, pottery for the tea ceremony and the lacquered floats of its Kunchi festival. It brings coastal Kyushu character, with a direct train to Fukuoka Airport.

The Appeal

Karatsu has a castle on a headland above a pine-fringed bay, one of Japan’s great tea-bowl traditions, a UNESCO-listed November festival, live squid at Yobuko and a through train to Fukuoka’s airport.

Why It Made the Shortlist

Karatsu has the mildest winters of the eight and reaches Fukuoka Airport in 75 to 99 minutes without changing trains. The Japan Seismic Hazard Information Station (J-SHIS) gives it a 3.3% chance of very strong shaking (intensity 6-lower on Japan’s scale) within 30 years.

Worth Knowing
  • The Genkai nuclear plant is about 15 km from the castle, and every district of the city lies within its planning zones.
  • The beachfront is exposed to tsunami, storm surge and typhoons.
Getting There

JR Chikuhi Line trains run straight onto the Fukuoka subway, reaching Fukuoka Airport in 75 to 99 minutes with no change, 11 times a day.

A Story From the Place

Tea people in Japan have an old saying that ranks the great tea bowls: first Raku, second Hagi, third Karatsu. Karatsu-yaki was begun by Korean potters who came to Kyushu, many of them by force, after Toyotomi Hideyoshi's invasions of Korea in the 1590s, which were launched from a castle just up the coast from here. Four centuries later, the town's kilns still fire the earthy, understated bowls that tea masters prize, and in 1988 the national government designated Karatsu-yaki a Traditional Craft.

At a Glance

Before You Decide

  • Check coastal, flood and slope maps, seasonal storms and the Genkai nuclear plant about 15 km from the castle.
Region / Prefecture
North-west Saga Prefecture, Kyushu, on Karatsu Bay and the Genkai Sea
Population
108,955 (1 August 2026, Saga Prefecture estimate based on the 2025 Census preliminary count; whole merged municipality) · 117,373 at the 2020 Census
Setting
Castle on a headland at the mouth of the Matsuura River · the Niji-no-Matsubara pine forest along the bay · hills and peninsulas behind
Heritage
Karatsu Kunchi float festival: national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property and UNESCO (2016, within Yama, Hoko, Yatai) · Karatsu-yaki pottery · Nagoya Castle ruins, Special Historic Site · Niji-no-Matsubara, Special Place of Scenic Beauty
What Makes It Distinct
A castle-and-sea town with living craft, on a direct train to an international airport
