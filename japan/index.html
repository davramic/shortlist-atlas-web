THE SHORTLIST

Get to Know
the Towns.

Start with the setting. Then read about daily life and the trade-offs. Every town opens a different possibility.

No. 01
Tsugaru, Aomori · Castle City and Apple Country

Hirosaki ↗

Cherry blossom fills the castle moat in spring, while samurai streets and apple orchards give Hirosaki depth beyond the season.

No. 02
San'in, Shimane · Lakeside City with a National Treasure Castle

Matsue ↗

Matsue has an original black castle, moats you can explore by boat and a lake beside the city.

No. 03
Hokuriku, Ishikawa · Gardens, Gold Leaf and the Shinkansen

Kanazawa ↗

Kanazawa has gardens, gold leaf, craft workshops and a food culture worth returning for.

No. 04
Kyushu, Saga · Castle, Bay and Tea Bowls Near Fukuoka Airport

Karatsu ↗

A castle above the bay, pottery for the tea ceremony and the lacquered floats of Kunchi.

No. 05
Tamba, Hyogo · Quiet Castle Town 90 Minutes from Osaka

Tamba-Sasayama ↗

Preserved castle-town streets and autumn food traditions draw weekend visitors from Kansai.

No. 06
Aizu, Fukushima · Samurai City and Red-Tiled Castle at Low Prices

Aizu-Wakamatsu ↗

A red-tiled castle and samurai history give Aizu-Wakamatsu a strong identity in inland Fukushima.

No. 07
Shiribeshi, Hokkaido · Historic Canal Port

Otaru ↗

Old warehouses line the canal, with the sea in front and snowy hills behind.

No. 08
Hida, Gifu · Old Merchant Town Below the Northern Alps

Takayama ↗

Timber merchant houses, sake breweries and festival craft fill Takayama’s old streets.
