Get to Know
the Towns.

Start with the setting. Then read about daily life and the trade-offs. Every town opens a different possibility.

No. 01
Piedmont · Wine Village on the Monferrato Ridge

Albugnano ↗

Vineyards, Vezzolano Abbey and views toward the Alps give Albugnano its appeal.

No. 02
Lombardy · Hilltop Balcony Over the Oltrepò Vineyards

Montalto Pavese ↗

Montalto Pavese is a ridge-top village above the vineyards of the Oltrepò Pavese.

No. 03
Tuscany · Mining Village in the Metal-Bearing Hills

Montieri ↗

Wooded hills and a history of mining shape this Tuscan town in the Colline Metallifere.

No. 04
Abruzzo · Brick Hill Town Between Mountains and Sea

Penne ↗

Brick streets climb an Abruzzo hill between mountain and Adriatic landscapes.

No. 05
Tuscany · Mountain Villages of the Garfagnana

Fabbriche di Vergemoli ↗

Stone villages sit beneath the Apuan landscape in this dispersed Garfagnana municipality.

No. 06
Emilia-Romagna · Hill Village in the Upper Valmarecchia

Casteldelci ↗

Casteldelci is a small municipality in the upper Valmarecchia, surrounded by the green hills of the Montefeltro.

No. 07
Liguria · Vara Valley Village Near the Cinque Terre

Pignone ↗

Stone streets in the Val di Vara offer a quieter inland base near the Cinque Terre.
