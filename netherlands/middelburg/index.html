Middelburg

The Island Capital of Zeeland, Between the Abbey, the Dunes and the Sea Defences

Zeeland · Island Capital Near Beaches and Delta Works
The Pitch

The capital of Zeeland, a medieval abbey town rebuilt after war at the heart of the old island of Walcheren, with beaches, dunes, mussels and the Delta Works close by, at prices well below the Randstad

Why It's No. 8

It is the coastal option on this list and good value, but it is, with Maastricht, the most remote from Schiphol, its 24-hour emergency department is in another town, and its sea-flood story has to be told straight

The Honest Cost

The nearest 24-hour emergency department is at the Adrz hospital in Goes, about 25 km away; Schiphol is about 2 h 30 by train; Walcheren is a low island behind dunes and dikes

Getting There

Direct intercity to Amsterdam in just under 3 h and to Rotterdam in about 1 h 40; Schiphol about 2 h 30 with a change; Rotterdam The Hague airport about 1 h 15 by road

THE SCORECARD

Why Middelburg Is Eighth

Middelburg is on this list because it offers something none of the other towns can: the sea. It is the capital of Zeeland, the province of islands and estuaries in the southwest, and it sits at the centre of the old island of Walcheren, a few kilometres from wide sandy beaches, dunes, and the great sea defences of the Delta Works. It has a medieval abbey that houses the provincial government, a skyline dominated by a tall abbey tower, a historic centre rebuilt after the destruction of 1940, and a relaxed, maritime way of life, at prices well below the Randstad. It ranks eighth for three plain reasons. Its 24-hour emergency department is not in Middelburg but at the Adrz hospital in Goes, about 25 kilometres away. Schiphol is about two and a half hours by train. And Walcheren is a low island protected by dunes and dikes, which is the sea-defence story this volume tells straight. For a buyer who wants the coast and accepts those terms, Middelburg is a delightful place to live.

A detail worth knowing

In 1608, a Middelburg spectacle-maker named Hans Lipperhey applied to the States General for a patent on an instrument "for seeing things far away as if they were nearby", the first recorded application for a telescope. The patent was refused, because others claimed the idea too, but news of the invention spread across Europe within months, and within a year Galileo had built his own and turned it to the sky. The telescope's first recorded appearance was in this small Zeeland town.

The Five Filters

Middelburg, Read Five Ways

Natural Hazard

LOW to MEDIUM. Walcheren is a low-lying island, historically dike ring 29, protected by dunes and sea dikes, and storm surge from the North Sea is the risk at national scale here. The island was deliberately flooded in 1944, and Zeeland was struck by the 1953 North Sea flood. The Delta Works and modern defence standards make a breach unlikely, and overstroomik.nl gives the modelled depth for each address. No seismic concern.

Access

LOW. With Maastricht, the most remote town on this list from Schiphol, about two and a half hours by train. Direct intercity trains run to Amsterdam, and Rotterdam The Hague airport is about an hour and a quarter by road for Europe.

Scenery and Culture

HIGH. The abbey and its tower, the Markt and the rebuilt old centre, the beaches and dunes of Walcheren, the Delta Works, and the harbour town of Veere nearby.

Rental Viability

MEDIUM. Strong summer beach tourism, but quiet winters, and holiday letting runs under the national registration framework, with platforms reporting every stay since May 2026.

Practical Living

LOW to MEDIUM. A small provincial capital with good everyday services, but the 24-hour emergency department is in Goes, about 25 km away, the weakest emergency-care distance of the eight, and major hospitals are further still.
