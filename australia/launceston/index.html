Launceston

The River City at the Head of the Tamar, with a Gorge in Its Backyard

Explore the Town ↓
Image & Source Notes ↗
Town Overview

Life in Launceston

Historic warehouses and shopfronts line the river, with Cataract Gorge close to town. Launceston adds a hospital, a university campus and an airport to its northern Tasmanian setting.

The Appeal

Launceston is a Georgian and Victorian river city of about 81,000 where three rivers meet, with Cataract Gorge a walk from the center, the Tamar Valley wine route on the doorstep and a UNESCO gastronomy designation to its name.

Why It Made the Shortlist

Launceston is the best all-round fit on the list. It has a real jet airport fifteen minutes out, a mild temperate climate and full regional-city services, plus new-housing areas at Legana, Prospect Vale and Hadspen, where a non-resident American can legally buy new.

Worth Knowing
  • Some low-lying flats sit behind levees that, on the latest climate modeling, could be overtopped by mid-century.
  • The island location adds one more flight to every trip home.
  • Established homes are closed to foreign buyers until at least mid-2029.
Getting There
  • Launceston Airport is about 15 to 20 minutes from the center, with direct flights to Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane.
  • Connect to the US through Melbourne or Sydney.
A Story From the Place

In 1895 Launceston switched on the Duck Reach power station in Cataract Gorge, which made it one of the first cities in the southern hemisphere to light its streets with hydroelectricity. The ruins of the station still stand in the gorge, a pleasant walk upstream from the First Basin, and the Tasmanian habit of turning river water into power that later remade the island's highlands started here.

At a Glance

Before You Decide

  • Foreign-buyer rules affect the type of home available. Check current eligibility, river flooding behind levees and bushfire exposure at the city edge.
State / Region
Tasmania · Northern Tasmania, at the head of the kanamaluka/Tamar estuary · City of Launceston, with growth fringes in West Tamar and Meander Valley councils
Population
80,943 (ABS 2021 Census, urban center) · a full regional city, the second largest in Tasmania
Setting
Where the North Esk and South Esk meet to form the Tamar · CBD and Invermay near sea level on river flats, suburbs climbing to roughly 100 to 200 m on the hills
Heritage
Founded 1806, among the oldest cities in Australia · Georgian, Victorian and Edwardian center · two UNESCO World Heritage convict estates at Longford, about 25 minutes away
What Makes It Distinct
UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy (2021) and a jet airport, in a city where you can still walk from the main street into a gorge
