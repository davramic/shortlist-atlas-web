BEAUTY IS THE BEGINNING

Then Come
the Better Questions.

What is it like to be here?
What would it take to live here?
Our dossiers look beyond the view.

01

Hazard

What the national registers say about earthquake, flood, wildfire and unstable ground, at the town's scale, and plainly what they cannot tell you about one parcel.

02

Access

The real way in and out: airports with scheduled service, mainline rail, the roads and the last slow mile, each marked for a live recheck.

03

Scenery and Culture

The specific draw that makes ordinary days worth living there. A strength of the place, never a safety claim.

04

Rental Viability

Whether lawful letting is possible at all, and whether demand outlasts the season. No yield is promised.

05

Practical Living

The life a postcard leaves out: emergency care, schools, markets, broadband, and a town that does not close for winter.

Official sources. Dated research. Plainly stated limits.Inside our approach ↗
