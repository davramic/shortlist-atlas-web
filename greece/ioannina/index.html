Ioannina

Lakefront Life and the Mountains of Epirus

Town Overview

Life in Ioannina

Ioannina offers lakefront walks, a historic center and the mountains of Epirus. It is a mainland alternative to the islands, with university life and substantial medical services.

The Appeal

Ioannina is a university city beside Lake Pamvotida, with an airport, motorway access and the Zagori cultural landscape nearby.

Why It Made the Shortlist

Ioannina makes a strong practical case: full year-round services, university-hospital care and Zone I, the lowest band of the Greek Seismic Code (EAK).

Worth Knowing

The low, closed basin has official flood mapping, and winters are cold and wet. Check drainage, basement condition and the Krya-spring water supply.

Getting There
  • King Pyrros Airport is a domestic gateway, mainly to Athens.
  • The Egnatia Odos motorway gives mainland road access, and Igoumenitsa has car ferries to Italy.
  • Check live schedules with the Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (HCAA), the airlines and the ferry operators.
A Story From the Place

For thirty-four years this provincial lake city was the capital of a court that rivaled Constantinople's. Ali Pasha of Tepelena ruled Epirus from Ioannina from 1788 to 1822, nominally as an Ottoman governor and actually as an independent despot. He kept his own foreign policy, negotiated with Napoleon and with Britain, and made this city one of the wealthiest and most cultured in the Balkans. In 1809 a twenty-one-year-old Lord Byron came through Ioannina on his way to meet him, and put him into Childe Harold. The Sultan eventually sent an army, and Ali Pasha was killed in 1822 on the little island in the middle of the lake, visible from the waterfront. They cornered him in a monastery there and shot him through the floorboards from the room below. His head went to Constantinople. You can still stand in the room, a ten-minute boat ride from the waterfront.

At a Glance

Before You Decide

  • The city is in Zone I of the Greek Seismic Code, but it lies in a mapped flood-risk basin. Check drainage, basements, ground floors and water-system reliability.
