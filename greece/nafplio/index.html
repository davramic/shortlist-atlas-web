THE SCORECARD

Why Nafplio, and on What Terms

Nafplio is the town you will want, and that is the problem this volume has to solve. It is the handsomest place on the Greek mainland: a Venetian harbour town under a fortress of eight bastions, with a castle on an island in the water in front of it, Epidaurus forty minutes east and the Lion Gate of Mycenae thirty minutes north. It was the first capital of the Greek state. It also sits on Seismic Zone I, the lowest band the Greek code recognises, which is a lower design assignment than several surrounding municipal units. Nafplio meets several shortlist criteria, subject to exact-address checks. Then you look at what it costs, and at where the water comes from.

A detail worth knowing

Everybody, including the guidebooks, will tell you the Palamidi has 999 steps. It has 857. Google's own data for the fortress says so, and so does anyone who has counted, and the round number survives because it is better as a story than as arithmetic. What is up there is more interesting than the myth anyway: the Venetians built the whole thing between 1711 and 1714, two hundred and sixteen metres above the town, as eight self-contained bastions, each one a fortress in its own right, designed so that if an enemy took one the others could still fire on it. The Greek state later renamed six of the eight for ancient generals, so a Venetian engineer's compartments now carry the names Themistocles, Miltiades, Achilles, Phocion, Epaminondas and Leonidas. The Five Filters

Nafplio, Read Five Ways

Low Hazard

Nafplio's former municipality is in EAK Zone I; Asini, Midea and Nea Tiryntha are in Zone II, so the exact parcel matters. Lerna springs are the principal water source and Anavalos supplements supply in drought. A July 2025 outage involved one of two Anavalos pumps and a main-pipe break, not dependence on one pump. Official flood-risk area EL03APSFR006 covers the Argos, Nafplio and Drepano low plain. Obtain parcel-level flood and drainage evidence. No local airport.

Access

Athens International is about 148 km away by road; KTEL intercity coaches connect via Athens. Verify the live KTEL timetable and road conditions rather than relying on a fixed journey-time promise.

Scenery / Culture

A compact historic town below Palamidi and Acronauplia, with Mycenae and Epidaurus nearby. Heritage status and opening arrangements should be checked with the Ministry of Culture.

Rental Viability

A potentially long season because of mainland access, but supply and regulation are mutable. Obtain current address-level comparable bookings and costs before using a yield assumption.

Practical Living

A compact, walkable mainland town with a general hospital and motorway access to Athens. Summer congestion, scarce parking, water resilience and flood exposure on the low plain are the practical constraints. Test the address in winter as well as August and verify that daily errands do not depend on a car.
