THE SHORTLIST

Get to Know
the Towns.

Start with the setting. Then read about daily life and the trade-offs. Every town opens a different possibility.

No. 01
Eastern Macedonia and Thrace · Historic Mainland Port

Kavala ↗

Kavala is a working port with hillside neighborhoods and layers of northern Greek history.

No. 02
Argolis · First Capital of Modern Greece

Nafplio ↗

Harbor walks beneath Venetian fortifications, with Epidaurus and Mycenae within day-trip reach.

No. 03
Cyclades · Island Capital of the Cyclades

Syros ↗

Marble squares and neoclassical streets rise above Ermoupoli’s working harbor, with Ano Syros on the hill.

No. 04
Cyclades · Green Island of the Shipowners

Andros ↗

Stone paths, springs and villages give Andros a greener, quieter side of Cycladic life.

No. 05
Cyclades · Largest Island of the Cyclades

Naxos ↗

Beach days give way to mountain villages, farming country and the Portara above the harbor.

No. 06
Epirus · Lakeside Capital of Epirus

Ioannina ↗

Ioannina offers lakefront walks and a historic center, with the mountains of Epirus within reach.

No. 07
Crete · Venetian Harbor Town

Chania ↗

Venetian harbor streets open toward the White Mountains, with beaches and villages beyond.

No. 08
Ionian Islands · Cosmopolitan Green Island

Corfu ↗

Fortresses frame a Venetian old town, with green Ionian scenery beyond it.

No. 09
Cyclades · Island of the Caldera

Santorini ↗

The caldera, vineyard landscapes and Akrotiri’s archaeology make Santorini an extraordinary place to explore.
