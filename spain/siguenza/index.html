Town Overview

Life in Sigüenza

A cathedral, a castle-Parador and an old town still lived in: Sigüenza rewards an unhurried visit, and its compact scale makes it a lovely, quieter Spanish home base.

The Appeal

Coffee below the castle-Parador, the cathedral bells overhead, and the wide open country of the Alto Henares a short walk from the old town.

Why It Made the Shortlist

Sigüenza makes the list as a cathedral town that is still lived in, small enough to know in a week, with rail and road links toward Madrid.

Worth Knowing

The upper and lower town behave differently, so check each one on its own:

  • The setting: river proximity, steep walking and winter conditions
  • Services: healthcare referral and train frequency
  • The building: parking and access
At a Glance

Before You Decide

  • Upper and lower districts differ. Check river exposure, steep routes, winter conditions, medical referrals, train frequency and access to the building.
