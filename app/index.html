BUILT TO EARN YOUR TRUST

The Useful Details.
the Honest Limits.

The right tool helps you make a decision. It also tells you what still needs checking.

What works offline?

Bundled country research and saved plans are designed to be readable on your phone. External maps, provider pages, tickets and live information need a connection.

Does it book travel or sell property?

The Atlas is a research and planning companion. Provider links open external services. No property earns its position by paying us a commission.

What about day counts and changing rules?

Stay history helps you record and review your dates. It does not determine your immigration status or tax residency. Research dates and source context should guide the checks you make with the relevant authority.

What do I get for free, and what is paid?

Free: browse all 14 countries and 116 places, explore attractions, take the interest quiz and plan one day. A 30-day Trip Pass unlocks the full planner for one trip. Traveler adds unlimited saved trips and travelling together. Atlas Member adds the complete country dossiers, Atlas Watch updates and Scouting Trips. Prices are shown in the app; purchases go through Apple.

Can I download it yet?

The app has been submitted to the App Store. The download link will appear here as soon as Apple approves it. In the meantime, every country shortlist and town profile is open to explore on this site.
